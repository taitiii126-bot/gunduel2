# itch.io に載せるときのメモ

このフォルダの `index.html` は、公式サイト（GitHub Pages）のゲームを枠の中に表示するだけの入り口です。
ゲームを更新すると itch.io 側も自動で最新になるので、アップロードし直す必要はありません。
公式サイトのアドレスが変わったときだけ、`index.html` の `GAME_URL`（2か所）を書きかえて、zip を作り直してアップロードします。

## Description（英語。そのまま貼れます）

**GUN DUEL** is a fast-paced 2D western shooter you can play right in your browser — no download.
Pick three weapons, read your opponent, dodge-roll through bullets and take them down.

**Features**
- Online battles: Ranked 1v1, 2v2, and private rooms to play with friends
- VS CPU with 5 difficulty levels (and a hidden boss)
- 15 weapons: pistol, revolver, sniper, shotgun, SMG, assault rifle, railgun, grenade launcher, bouncing balls, knife, hammer and more
- 6 stages with different layouts
- Customize your gunslinger: hats, outfits, neckwear, emotes and victory poses
- Daily missions, season pass, titles, clans and seasonal events
- Quick chat with preset phrases
- Plays on PC (keyboard or gamepad) and smartphones (landscape)

**Controls (PC, can be changed in Settings)**
- Move: A / D　Jump: W　Crouch: S
- Shoot: Space　Reload: R　Switch weapon: 1 / 2 / 3　Heal: 4
- Dodge roll: Shift　Emotes: Z / X / C / V　Quick chat: T

**About this page**
This page shows the official game site, so it is always the latest version.
Discord login does not work inside this frame — press the login button and the official site opens in a new tab, where you can log in to keep your profile on every device and use ranked matches and friends.

The game is available in English and Japanese (日本語).
